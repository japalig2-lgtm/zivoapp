Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and personal data.

To request account deletion:

  1. Send an email to zivo.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use your registered phone number.
  3. Mention “Delete my account”.

Your account and associated data will be deleted within 7 days of the request.

Data deleted includes profile information, call history and app usage data. Some data may be retained if required for legal or security purposes.
